Subject: optional signing-identity field in your server manifest — worth it or not?

Hello,

I maintain MCP servers listed on your directory. This is a schema question, not a placement
request.

We have drafted AWR/2: signed claims about AI work. A WorkReceipt records what its issuer says
ran; a VerificationVerdict is a verifier signing its own judgement about one specific receipt, so
the verdict is attributable to a named key. Neither establishes that the work happened — the
signature makes the claim attributable and tamper-evident, and that is all. Both are W3C VC 2.0
with an eddsa-jcs-2022 proof over RFC 8785. Verification is offline by specification — no fetch,
not even for @context.

The question: would an optional manifest field declaring the did:key a server signs receipts
with be useful to you, or a field nobody reads?

Today the honest answer may be the second. AWR/2 has no production emitters, and a key no
client verifies is decoration. "Come back when something emits" is a useful answer.
