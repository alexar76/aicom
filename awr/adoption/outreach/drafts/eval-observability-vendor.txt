Subject: giving your judge output an identity — one question about VerificationVerdict

Hello,

If your judge output is unsigned JSON, it cannot show a customer's auditor which service produced
the score, and it cannot be compared with another judge's claim about the same output. If you
already sign it, this note is not for you — we have not examined your product and are not
asserting anything about it.

AWR/2 addresses that gap. A VerificationVerdict is a W3C VC 2.0 credential signed with your own
did:key. It references the work it judged by digest as well as identifier, so a consumer that
recomputes the digest can detect a favourable verdict re-attached to different work; without that
recomputation the substitution is undetectable and every signature still checks. It names its
method by an opaque id, so two verdicts are comparable only when they claim the same method.
"inconclusive" is a first-class outcome that must not be reported as failure. Verification needs
no network.

The ask: sign the verdicts you already produce, with your own key, and show the issuer identity
next to the score.

The strongest objection is yours: signing makes your judgements quotable against you in a
dispute. That is the point, and a real cost.
