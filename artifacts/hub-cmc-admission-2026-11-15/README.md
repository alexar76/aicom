## Outcome, 19 November 2026

CoinMarketCap answered ticket 1474981 twice. On 17 November Grace (API Support) confirmed that the route is served through x402 and that x402 access stays under the Commercial API Terms. On 19 November, after checking with their team, she wrote that the pilot is not permitted under the standard licence: x402 support "does not grant permission to redistribute, sublicense, or expose CoinMarketCap market data through third-party platforms or callable API routes", even for two USD quotes with attribution. The reply did not name a team for a separate agreement, did not answer the offer of a listing operated by CoinMarketCap, and did not confirm the recipient `0x3C5f3a6cE224BB89D72f5EB4232ecC27F67B3eeA`; it repeated only the USDC contract address.

Decision: no callable CoinMarketCap route on any Hub. Do not admit it to the catalogue, do not configure it as an observed route, and do not fund the bridge for it. The production canary above is cancelled.

Checked on the live `modelmarket.dev` the same day: `/ai-market/v2/external-services` lists CoinMarketCap only as a reference to its own documentation and endpoint, with `hub_invoke: false` and `payment_recipient: provider`, so an agent pays CoinMarketCap directly and the Hub never fetches or relays the quotes. The bridge reported a balance of 0 USDC, nothing spent today and no recent purchases. The CMC settlement check in `x402_bridge.py` stays, since it only runs on a route that was admitted.
