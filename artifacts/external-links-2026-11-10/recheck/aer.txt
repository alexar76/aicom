How this is computed
Adoption weighted by
organic
paying agents (wallets that also pay
other services; captive/faucet wallets don't count), real depurated settlement, PageRank on the
organic payment sub-graph (so fake wallets can't buy a rating), gated by uptime. The trust line is
BBB. Outlook tracks the adoption trend week over week. A service is one entry regardless of how
many networks it settles on. A listed resource that answers
410 Gone
with a
Sunset
date already past (RFC 8594) is a declared retirement: it leaves the uptime
denominator instead of counting as down. A bare 410, a 404 or a 5xx counts as down.
Coverage:
services listed in the x402 discovery catalogs (CDP Bazaar and PayAI), plus unlisted wallets seen settling on-chain;
settlement measured in USDC on Base, Polygon, Arbitrum and Solana mainnets.
