Junjie, all, We implemented the manifest and lookup parts on the AIMarket hubs we run, as another way into catalogues that otherwise speak our own protocol: /.well-known/ard.json, the "ard" link relation, POST /search, POST /explore and GET /agents, with publisher trustManifests under did:web. For example:
https://modelmarket.dev/.well-known/ard.json
.
https://modelmarket.dev/.well-known/ard.json
. How our catalogue maps onto ARD entries:
https://github.com/alexar76/aimarket-hub/blob/main/docs/ard.md
.
https://github.com/alexar76/aimarket-hub/blob/main/docs/ard.md
. Some notes from doing that: 1. The CDDL in section 5 of draft-bu-dawn-ard-discovery-00 does not compile with the cddl tool: it redefines "any" and "null", which the RFC 8610 prelude already defines. With those lines removed, and the url/data group choice written as two map alternatives (the tool stops on it inside the entry map), our manifest, a list page, a search request and a search response validate. Because of the open "* tstr => any" member, the CDDL cannot express that an entry carries exactly one of url and data. 2. Prose and CDDL differ on what is required: the trustManifest "subject" and "signature" are MUST in section 3.5 and optional in the CDDL, and "query.text" is MUST for /search (4.2.2.1) but optional in "query-model". 3. The DNS owner names differ between the documents: initial-contact 4.2.1 uses
_index._agents.example.com
, while discovery 4.1 refers to _entries._agents and _search._agents. 4. "Detached JWS" could say whether the payload enters the signing input base64url-encoded (RFC 7515, Appendix F) or unencoded (RFC 7797, b64=false). A test vector would settle it. We used Appendix F. 5. ES256 is mandatory to implement. A publisher whose key is Ed25519 signs with EdDSA, and an ES256-only verifier will reject it. Making EdDSA (RFC 8037) mandatory to implement as well would spare such publishers a second key. 6. (identifier, version) is the deduplication key, but "version" is optional and rarely moves in practice: in our records of public MCP servers, 81.3% of tool-definition changes kept the same serverInfo.version
https://histor.modelmarket.dev/datasets/mcp-protocol-eras-2026-11-01/
)
https://histor.modelmarket.dev/datasets/mcp-protocol-eras-2026-11-01/
) The digest is the reliable change signal and might deserve a SHOULD. 7. GET /agents: the response body and the filter syntax in the query string are not specified. We return {"entries": [...], "nextPageToken": ...} and read a repeated parameter as OR. 8. search-result-item has no version member, while a signed result's trustManifest subject includes version and section 3.5.1 has verifiers match the subject against the entry. A registry that returns only the listed members leaves its signed results unverifiable; we ran into this in our first deployment. Adding "? version" to search-result-item would close it.
