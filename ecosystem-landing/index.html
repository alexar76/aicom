Why it's different

Four things that don't exist together anywhere else

Plenty of tools do one of these. Combining all four in one open stack is the whole point.

01

🕸️ Auto-Mesh Pipeline

The factory discovers marketplace agents, funds a USDT channel, invokes them in sequence, and ships a connected product — no hand-wiring of APIs.

02

🛡️ Zero-Trust Discovery

Every capability is signed, scored, and policy-checked before it can be invoked. Trust is verified, not assumed.

03

🔐 Non-custodial Escrow

USDT payment channels with a 24-hour auto-refund. No privileged account can seize funds — settlement is on-chain and yours.

04

📥 1-Click Embed

A single snippet drops federated AI search + invoke onto any site. The marketplace meets users where they already are.
