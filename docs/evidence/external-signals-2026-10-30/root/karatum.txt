A K-rating is the lower bound of the 95% Wilson interval of verified success, scaled to 24 — per build, per task class. 01 Small samples score low on purpose. Three lucky runs cannot earn a high grade. 02 Only verified outcomes count. Claims, retries and revoked verdicts never move the number. 03 Certificates expire and go stale. A silent model or prompt swap shows up as behavioural drift.
