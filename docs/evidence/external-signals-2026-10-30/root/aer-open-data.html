The daily archive, as files

Everything on this site is computed every morning from the same archive. This page publishes that archive: the rating of every x402 service for every day since 2026-08-16, the daily Agent Economy Index since 2026-08-02, a daily census of the x402 discovery catalog, a daily liveness census of every resource ever listed (of those listed that day, and of the listed hosts) and the rating of every agent skill in the ClawHub registry. Same numbers as the pages, same day, with a schema and a citation; the monthly report's totals (gross flow, flow at agent scale, settlement transfers, listed and dead resources) are sums over these files: in aei-daily.csv each closed monthly window (the 2nd of a month to the 1st of the next, the window of the monthly report) is computed with the wallet classification of that report date (so the report's totals are sums of these rows), and the current window with today's, as the AEI page shows it. Method: methodology.

Columns

ratings-daily.csv.gz

day date
Rating day (UTC morning run).
host string
Service host as listed in the x402 catalogs, or the payTo wallet for unlisted entries.
kind string
service (host) or unlisted_wallet.
tier string
AAA to D; BBB is the trust line.
score number
Score 0 to 100 (40 adoption, 25 settlement, 25 centrality, 10 uptime).
outlook string
positive, negative, stable or new (trend of organic agents over 7 days).
flag string
CAPTIVE, SYBIL, NEW, RETIRED or empty.
uptime number
Share of the daily probes in the 14-day window answered as up (402 or 200, see the methodology), 0 to 1. From 2026-11-02 (D-0061) only the resources listed in a catalog in the last 7 days count when one of them has a probe that counts (for a family, only the endpoints it lists), and a 405 never answered by POST counts as down, as does a probe without an answer except on a day the probe itself failed (before, both were left out).
organic_payers integer
Distinct organic paying wallets in the 14-day window (scanners excluded).
payers integer
Distinct paying wallets in the window, scanners included.
organic_payers_7d integer
Organic payers in the last 7 days of the window.
organic_payers_7d_prev integer
Organic payers in the 7 days before that.
settled_usd_14d number
USDC settled to the entry in 14 days at agent scale (a payment counts at most US$ 1).
centrality number
PageRank-style centrality in the organic payment graph.
retired_resources integer
Listed resources that answered 410 with a past Sunset date.
family integer
Family id when several hosts share the payTo wallet (D-0050); empty otherwise.
tier_raw string
Tier the day's numbers alone would give; differs from tier only when the 2-point band holds the previous day's tier (D-0058, from 2026-10-22); empty on days rated before the column existed.

day date
Day (UTC).
usd number
USDC settled to x402 services at agent scale (a payment counts at most US$ 1).
gross number
USDC settled before the agent-scale cap (gross flow).
tx integer
Number of settlement transfers counted.

catalog-daily.csv

day date
Snapshot day.
resources integer
Resources listed in the CDP discovery catalog that day.
hosts integer
Distinct hosts among those resources.
paid_calls_30d integer
Sum of the 30-day paid-call counts the catalog reports per resource (gross, as declared).
payers_30d integer
Sum of the 30-day distinct-payer counts the catalog reports per resource.

liveness-daily.csv

day date
Probe day (UTC).
probed integer
Resources probed that day: every resource ever listed in the x402 discovery catalogs (CDP Bazaar and PayAI) since 2 August 2026, including those no longer listed.
answered_402 integer
Of those, resources that answered HTTP 402 with a price (alive and selling).
dead integer
Of those, resources that did not answer at all or answered a 5xx (no response, connection error or server error).
listed integer
Resources probed that day that were listed in the catalogs that same day (the census in catalog-daily.csv). Empty on a day without a catalog census.
listed_answered_402 integer
Of the listed ones, resources that answered HTTP 402 with a price. Empty on a day without a catalog census.
listed_dead integer
Of the listed ones, resources that did not answer at all or answered a 5xx. Empty on a day without a catalog census.
listed_hosts integer
Hosts with at least one listed resource probed that day. Empty on a day without a catalog census.
listed_hosts_answered_402 integer
Of those hosts, the ones with at least one listed resource answering HTTP 402 with a price that day: the liveness by host, which one host with many resources cannot move by itself. Empty on a day without a catalog census.
largest_host string
The host with the most listed resources probed that day. Empty on a day without a catalog census.
largest_host_share_pct number
Share (%) of the listed resources probed that day that belong to largest_host: how much the liveness by resource depends on one host. Empty on a day without a catalog census.

What is not here

The raw payment graph (payer wallet, payTo wallet, day, amount) and the raw liveness probes are not published as files: they identify wallets at a granularity the pages do not show. They are available to researchers on request, under the same licence, with citation. Write to contact@agenteconomy.report.

This is an operational trust rating of a software service computed from public, on-chain and liveness data: not a credit rating and not investment advice. Every figure is reproducible from our daily archive.
