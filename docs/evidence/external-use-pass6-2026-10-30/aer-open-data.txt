The daily archive, as files
Everything on this site is computed every morning from the same archive. This page publishes that archive:
the rating of every x402 service for every day since 2026-08-16, the daily Agent Economy Index since
2026-08-02, a daily census of the x402 discovery catalog, a daily liveness census of every resource ever
listed (of those listed that day, and of the listed hosts) and the rating of every agent skill in the ClawHub registry. Same numbers as the pages, same day, with a schema and
a citation; the monthly report's totals (gross flow, flow at agent scale, settlement transfers, listed and dead
resources) are sums over these files: in
aei-daily.csv
each closed monthly window (the 2nd of a month
to the 1st of the next, the window of the monthly report) is computed with the wallet classification of that report
date (so the report's totals are sums of these rows), and the current window with today's, as the AEI page
shows it. Method:

Service host as listed in the x402 catalogs, or the payTo wallet for unlisted entries.

AAA to D; BBB is the trust line.

Score 0 to 100 (40 adoption, 25 settlement, 25 centrality, 10 uptime).
outlook

positive, negative, stable or new (trend of organic agents over 7 days).
flag
string
CAPTIVE, SYBIL, NEW, RETIRED or empty.
uptime
number
Share of the daily probes in the 14-day window answered as up (402 or 200, see the methodology), 0 to 1. From 2026-11-02 (D-0061) only the resources listed in a catalog in the last 7 days count when one of them has a probe that counts (for a family, only the endpoints it lists), and a 405 never answered by POST counts as down, as does a probe without an answer except on a day the probe itself failed (before, both were left out).
organic_payers

USDC settled to the entry in 14 days at agent scale (a payment counts at most US$ 1).

PageRank-style centrality in the organic payment graph.

family

Family id when several hosts share the payTo wallet (D-0050); empty otherwise.

Of those, resources that did not answer at all or answered a 5xx (no response, connection error or server error).

listed_dead

listed_hosts

listed_hosts_answered_402

What is not here
The raw payment graph (payer wallet, payTo wallet, day, amount) and the raw liveness probes are not published
as files: they identify wallets at a granularity the pages do not show. They are available to researchers on
request, under the same licence, with citation. Write to contact@agenteconomy.report.

operational trust rating
of a software service computed from public, on-chain and liveness data:
not a credit rating
and not investment advice. Every figure is reproducible from our daily archive.
