# CRM / Sales pipeline — playbook

> **Built-in pack:** `crm_sales` — see [README.md](./README.md) and [methodology-agent.md](../methodology-agent.md).

- Every lead and deal needs a clear **owner** and **stage**; avoid free-text-only status.
- Log **activities** (calls, emails, meetings) on the record, not only notes.
- Pipeline reports must reconcile to **stage counts** and **conversion** between stages.
- Forecasts should separate **probability × amount** from committed revenue.
- Enforce **lost reasons** so reporting is actionable, not “lost” with no detail.
